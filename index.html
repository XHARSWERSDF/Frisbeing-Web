01   Who we are

Founded by

frisbee folks

We are the Tsinglan Frisbeeing Club — founded all the way back in 2022 by a handful of students who could not stop throwing.

What started as a shared obsession with ultimate is now a full roster, built on three things we refuse to compromise on: collaboration, teamwork and sportsmanship.

Ultimate has no referees. Every call on the field is made by the players themselves — so every point doubles as a lesson in honesty, in disagreeing well, and in trusting whoever is marking you.

03   The plan

What we
plan to do

01

Run the competitions

We host and enter frisbee tournaments built around the qualities we actually rate — fair play, full-effort running, and calling your own fouls. Winning is good. How you played for it counts double.

02

Train every single week

Two nights of live game play and one dedicated tactical session. Throws, cuts, stack discipline, defensive reads — skills that compound week on week until they are instinct.

04   The impact

What we
give back

Ultimate is one of the only competitive sports that polices itself. No referees, no officials — just players holding each other to the standard. That is the most useful thing this club puts into Tsinglan.

01

Everyone plays

We take complete beginners. No trials, no cuts, no bench — if you turn up, you are on.

02

Honesty under pressure

Self-refereeing forces students to make fair calls against their own interest, then argue it out and move on.

03

One mixed roster

Boys and girls train and compete together — same drills, same points, same field.

SHUL · Undefeated

Three evenings a week the field is loud and anyone who wants in is already in. When we travel to tournaments we carry the school's name with us — and we bring it back the way we found it.
